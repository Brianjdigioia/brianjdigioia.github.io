Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Personal TikTok Upload Tool is a personal tool used only by its developer with his own TikTok account.

What data is used

Through TikTok Login Kit, the tool receives an access token for the developer's own TikTok account (scopes: user.info.basic and video.upload). The token is used only to upload the developer's own videos to the drafts inbox of that account.

Where data is stored

The access token and the videos remain on the developer's own computer. No data is collected from other people, and nothing is stored on this website or on any server.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with or disclosed to third parties, other than the video files sent to TikTok to be uploaded to the developer's own account.
